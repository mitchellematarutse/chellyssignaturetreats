Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?
We recommend placing custom cake orders at least 5–7 business days in advance. For wedding cakes or very large orders, please allow 2–3 weeks so we can give your creation the time and attention it deserves.
Do you offer gluten-free or allergen-friendly options?
We are working on introducing allergen-friendly options. Please contact us directly to discuss your dietary requirements and we will do our best to accommodate you.
Which areas do you deliver to in Cape Town?
We currently deliver across greater Cape Town. Delivery fees vary by suburb and are calculated at checkout. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, please contact us and we'll confirm.
Can I make changes to my order after placing it?
Changes to orders can be accommodated if made at least 48 hours before your requested delivery or pickup date. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to adjust anything.
How do I earn and redeem loyalty points?
You earn 1 loyalty point for every R10 spent. Points can be redeemed at checkout for discounts, free treats, or to send a gift surprise to someone you love. Visit our Loyalty & Rewards page for the full breakdown.
