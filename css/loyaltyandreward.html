Getting Started

How It Works

  1. Place Your Order

    Browse our menu, add your favourite treats to your cart, and complete your purchase. Every rand you spend earns you loyalty points automatically.

  2. Watch Your Points Grow

    Earn 1 point for every R10 spent. Special occasions and seasonal promotions often include bonus point events — keep an eye on our social media for announcements.

  3. Redeem Your Rewards

    At checkout, apply your points balance to unlock discounts, free treats, or send a gift to someone special. Choose how you want to use them — it's your reward.

  4. Send a Sweet Surprise

    Use your points to send a beautifully packaged Chelly's treat box directly to a friend or loved one. Fill in their details below and we'll handle the rest.
